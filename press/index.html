About, ready to copy

Best Fun Evening is a family-made collection of party games for one phone: pass it around and play, with no cards, no setup and no accounts. The Best Fun Evening app brings all the games of the evening and a party kit together in one place, and five of the games also come out as separate apps for iPhone and Android in December 2026.

The app

Best Fun Evening: Party Games

The evening you can't put down

The whole evening in one app. Games, quick decisions and laughs on one phone that gets passed around. Every section has its own host character, and one Evening Pass unlocks everything and removes ads. Kiss, Marry, Ghost is not part of it, so the app suits family evenings too.

The five games

Each also comes out as a separate app. Links open the game in the browser.

Best Fun Evening Plan!

Clue quiz for family & friends

Pick a theme, guess from clues and score points all evening. Four difficulty levels, from relaxed to expert.

1–10 players · all ages · Hosted by the Grandpa

bfep.space/bfep

Bluff Night

Find the bluffer

Everyone gets the same secret word except one player. Talk, ask, vote and catch the bluffer. Play on one phone or on everyone's phones by QR code.

3–10 players · friends & parties · Hosted by the Elegant Mum

bfep.space/bluff

Three Clues Daily

One puzzle a day for everyone

A new guessing puzzle every midnight, the same for everyone. Five clues, five guesses, keep your streak going.

Solo · one puzzle a day · Hosted by the Bookworm

bfep.space/daily

Say It!

Questions that get everyone talking

1,500 party questions: Would You Rather, Most Likely To, Never Have I Ever, Truth or Dare and more, in Family, Friends 16+ and Adults 18+ moods.

2–10 players · family, friends 16+, adults 18+ · Hosted by the Strong Dad

bfep.space/sayit

Kiss, Marry, Ghost

Three options. No take-backs.

Everyone secretly decides who to kiss, marry and ghost, then you see who thinks alike. Cheeky, never explicit. Separate app only.

2–10 players · 16+

To show and measure ads, the ad SDK processes: the advertising identifier of your device (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, where permitted), IP address and approximate (city-level) location derived from it, device type, operating system, language, App version, and your interactions with ads (views, clicks). With your consent, ads may be personalised based on this data; without consent you receive non-personalised ads, which still use the IP address and a technical identifier for frequency capping and fraud prevention. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

2.3 Subscription (ad-free)

Purchases are made through Apple App Store, Google Play or Huawei AppGallery. The store processes your payment and identity; we receive only an anonymous confirmation that a subscription is active. We never see your card details.

The App has no account, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting and no tracking of its own.
